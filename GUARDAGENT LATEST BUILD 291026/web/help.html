Urgent

Scammed or threatened? Do this now

If someone is threatening you in person, your safety comes first: hand over what you must and call emergency services (999 in the UK, 911 in the US, 112 in the EU) as soon as it's safe.

  1. Stop all contact with the scammer.Don't send more money, don't sign anything, and don't click new links they send.
  2. Secure what's left.Move remaining funds to a brand-new wallet, revoke token approvals, and change passwords and 2FA on linked accounts.
  3. Report it to the police.US: ic3.gov. England, Wales and Northern Ireland: reportfraud.police.uk. Scotland: call 101. Exchanges and stablecoin issuers act faster with a police reference.
  4. Get emergency help.SEAL 911 is a free volunteer team for active crypto thefts. They will never ask for your seed phrase or a fee.
  5. Send freeze requests fast.Freeze Desk locks your evidence and sends pre-formatted freeze requests to exchanges for you, then keeps tracing the funds.
  6. Ignore "recovery" offers.Anyone who contacts you offering to recover your crypto for a fee is running a second scam.

Your agent and alerts

How do I tell my agent what to protect?

Open the dashboard, go to Briefing, and add your public wallet addresses, emails and domains. Never paste a seed phrase or private key anywhere.

I'm not getting alerts.

Check Alert channels in the dashboard: connect Telegram (press Start in the bot) or add an alert email. Medium alerts go to Telegram; high and critical also go to email and push.

How do I pair the browser extension?

In the dashboard, open Browser extension, press Get pairing code, then enter your Bodyguard address and the 8-digit code in the extension within 10 minutes.

Duress Mode and Freeze Desk

How does Duress Mode work?

You set a real PIN and a duress PIN. Under threat, open your vault and enter the duress PIN: it shows only a small decoy wallet, silently emails your trusted contacts, and holds large transfers protected by Bodyguard for your cooling-off period. Nobody, including Bodyguard, can end the time-lock early.

Can Freeze Desk get my money back?

No one can promise that. Freeze Desk gives you the best chance by moving fast: it locks your evidence, sends pre-formatted freeze requests to exchanges and responders, and traces where the funds go. Anyone who charges a fee to "recover" stolen crypto is running a second scam.
