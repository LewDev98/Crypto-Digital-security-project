        FAQ

        Questions before you hire

        Will my agent ever need my seed phrase?

        No. Never. Wallet monitoring only needs your public addresses. If anyone claiming to be Bodyguard asks for a seed phrase or private key, it's a scam.

        Can the agent stop a transaction?

        It doesn't hold keys, so it can't freeze or move funds. What it does is spot the risk early, alert you in seconds and hand you the exact next step, like a revoke link for a bad approval.

        Which chains are covered?

        Solana, Ethereum and Bitcoin at the core, plus Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon and BNB Chain. More L2s are added as people use them.

        How does it watch my email and accounts?

        You connect the accounts you want covered. The agent reads security signals like new sign-ins, forwarding rules and password resets, and checks your addresses against new breach data.

        How do alerts reach me?

        Telegram, email or push, depending on your tier. Each alert says what happened, how serious it is, and what to do next.

        What is the official $GUARD contract address?

        $GUARD is live on Solana. The only official contract address is Hx6iP5hivj8Ezr5gENBdSioZVhkXo8KArLQc1tnGpump. Treat any other address as fake.

        Can it stop spam tokens and scam cNFTs arriving?

        No one can. On Solana, Ethereum and Bitcoin anyone can send anything to any address. Spam is only dangerous when you interact with it, so your agent flags it, the shield blocks sends to poisoned addresses, and the spam sweep burns the junk and returns its rent.

        Are services part of the ranks?

        No. Guarded Wallets, Treasury Watch, Risk Screening, Scam Drills, Duress Mode and Freeze Desk are sold separately in USDC plus $GUARD, per 30 days. The $GUARD is burnt in the same way, and the USDC covers the cost of running the service. Prices for Duress Mode and Freeze Desk are announced soon.

        What is Duress Mode?

        Protection against "wrench attacks", where someone forces you to send your crypto. You set a second PIN. Entering it opens a decoy vault with a small balance, silently emails your trusted contacts, and holds large transfers for a cooling-off period that nobody can shorten. Switching it off only takes effect 24 hours later.

        I've just been scammed. What should I do?

        Act fast: freeze requests work best in the first hours. Stop all contact with the scammer, never pay anyone who promises to "recover" funds, report it to the police, and contact SEAL 911 for free emergency help. Freeze Desk locks your evidence and sends pre-formatted freeze requests to exchanges for you. Our help page has the full checklist.

        What happens to the fees I pay?

        The $GUARD part of every fee is burnt and removed from supply. The SOL part covers the cost of running your agent.
